## Trend 2: “Why Would I Deceive You?”

In September 2026, A24 dropped the trailer for Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder’s documentary, _You Can See Everything._ The nearly three-hour film captures the weeks before businesswoman Elizabeth Holmes’ incarceration, dominating discourse in a way usually reserved for Netflix true-crime shows.

It’s also produced a meme for the ages. The trailer’s stand-out moment is a wide-eyed Holmes, asking Fielder “Why would I deceive you?” when her authenticity is questioned. Given she was convicted of fraud, TikTok now uses this as an ironic shorthand for anything that feels too good to be true. It’s still popping off now, featuring in a _Saturday Night Live_ sketch just last week.

## Trend 4: Your first time in a fancy cafe

Making eye contact on the New York Subway. Trying to pay with cash in Sweden. Shrek drinking from the finger bowl instead of washing his hands in it. It’s hard to understand these unwritten social contracts.

Take your first time in a fancy cafe. You see the brutalist architecture and the steel table that looks _exactly_ like a stool, so you make an educated guess. You sit on it. Someone else sits next to you, using the table as an actual table. Oops.

This found its feet as an AI-generated meme, in which TikTok users inserted themselves (and sometimes celebrities). The trend’s since evolved, now covering any and all weirdly placed furniture.
