What are trending TikTok sounds?

Trending TikTok sounds are songs, remixes, voiceovers, or original audio that creators are using in new posts right now. The useful ones give you a format people already understand, so you can spend more time on your angle and less time explaining the joke.

The list at the top of this page is the current shortlist — the sounds creators are picking up now, with the newest arrivals flagged. It refreshes weekly, and the date above the list tells you when it was last checked. Members get the full top 20 refreshed daily.

Can you use trending TikTok sounds for business?

Not always. Popular music on TikTok is generally cleared for personal accounts only, and business accounts are limited to TikTok's Commercial Music Library. Original audio and voiceovers are a different question again, since the rights sit with whoever made them. If you post from a business account, check the sound is available to you inside the app before you build a video around it.

How do I find trending sounds on Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts?

Both platforms flag trending audio inside their own creation tools — Reels marks trending tracks with an arrow, and Shorts surfaces popular audio in its sound picker. Kurrently is focused on TikTok, so this page won't tell you what's moving on Reels or Shorts. Sounds do often cross over from TikTok first, so an early TikTok sound is a reasonable thing to test elsewhere.

How do I find trending TikTok sounds early?

Look for repeat use across smaller and mid-size creators, not just one huge video. If you keep seeing the same sound used in different ways, it may still have room. Kurrently does that scan for you and keeps the list focused on sounds that are still moving.

Do trending sounds help you go viral?

They help, but they do not carry a weak idea. A good sound gives your post a familiar rhythm. Your hook, take, edit, and niche still decide whether people watch.
