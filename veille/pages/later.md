### **Trend:** **Appointment timestamps** **— November 13, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** The day-in-the-life timestamp trend, but make it one appointment. Instead of your whole day, take us from walking in to walking out, whether it’s a manicure, a facial, or a fresh cut. Especially great for service-based businesses, because it’s basically a satisfying behind-the-scenes in disguise.

**Audio:** [Original audio](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/27734209222913191/)

### **Trend: Not every angel — November 06, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** Not every angel can walk the VSFS runway, and that’s the whole trend. Creators are pairing this audio with the on-screen text “Not every angel can walk VSFS, some of us need to…” and finishing it with something way less glamorous (and way more relatable). It’s been all over our feeds since the show, and lowkey, we’re here for it.

**Audio:** [Angel of my dreams – Jade](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/496336559627131/)

### **Trend: I just wanna — November 06, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** “I just wanna make a lot of money and…” Now finish the sentence. Creators are setting Tate McRae’s “Housewife” over B-roll of their lifestyle, then revealing exactly how they’d spend the cash. It’s equal parts manifestation and mood board, and a great fit for lifestyle creators and brands selling an aspirational vibe.

**Audio:** [Housewife – Tate McRae](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/805858655954254/)

### **Trend: Every girl for absolutely no reason — November 06, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** Picture this: you’re in your PJs, doom-scrolling, and then, for absolutely no reason, you’re up and trying on a brand-new fit. This one’s giving fashion haul, but make it playful. Perfect for fashion and lifestyle creators (or brands) showing off their latest buys.

**Audio:** [The Blue Danube Waltz – Royal Symphony Orchestra](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/2488033034771649/)

## **What are the Trending Reels on Instagram? — October 2026 Roundup**

Discover the hottest trends on Instagram Reels this month.

### **Trend: Small text zoom — October 30, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** Small text, big feelings. Use it for an encouraging message, a hint at an announcement, or something your audience will relate to. Swap in your own text, and it works for any niche.

**Audio:** [Drop It Like It’s Hot x Staying Alive](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/35471216759159857/)

### **Trend: Recharged — October 30, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** Battery at 1%? Not for long. Take your audience from 1% to 100% with a before and after that shows a glow-up, a mood switch, or an energy boost. It’s big in beauty and wellness, but it works for any transformation. Your recharge can be a best friend, a product, or a service.

**Audio:** [Original audio](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/39477063311941741/)

### **Trend: Toggle on — October 30, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** News alert: toggle on. This is an easy way to tease an announcement, a news post, or a new series on your channel. It’s simple to recreate and works for any niche.

**Audio:** [Mix – sviola\_handmade](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/29285135541104810/)

### **Trend: Refuse to be limited by a single niche — October 23, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** Niche? Never heard of her. Open with the statement, then run through your many interests one by one, with a rapid-fire photo dump between each. It’s made for lifestyle creators and personal brands who want to show there’s more to them than one content lane.

**Audio:** [Training Season – Dua Lipa](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/2085508281903593/)

### **Trend: Vlog timestamps — October 23, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** Your whole day, one swipe-right transition at a time. Each clip gets a timestamp, from the morning routine and breakfast to the workday and everything in between, with no voiceover needed. Lifestyle creators, personal brands, and service providers, this is an easy way to make your daily routine feel like a mini vlog.

**Audio:** [Pump It – The Black Eyed Peas](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/1154559479569864/)

### **Trend: My reaction — October 23, 2026**

**Trend Recap:** Your face is the ranking system. Pick something from your niche, then give your honest opinion using only facial expressions. Super easy to film, and it gets people arguing in the comments.

**Audio:** [Original audio](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/28278178948540945/)
